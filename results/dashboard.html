Signal
How far CYPH's price relative to ZEC, ln(CYPH ÷ ZEC), sits from its average over the last 70 hourly bars (about two trading weeks), in standard deviations (the z-score). Checked at each hourly close during US market hours.
Normally

CYPH cheap
z below −1.5: move to 100% CYPH.
ZEC cheap
z above +1.5: move to 100% ZEC.
Back to 50/50
When z crosses 0 (the gap has closed), or as a stop-loss if z stretches to ±3.0. After a stop, don't tilt the same way again until z is back inside ±1.5.
Execution
Trade at the next hour's opening price. No shorting, no borrowing. Costs assumed: 0.2% per side.
